Collection 23 · Spring 2026

Ten years ago, India gave us
our first fabric. This collection
gives it back everything.

Our 23rd collection is a love letter to the country that has been part of Merlette since the beginning. The artisans in Rajasthan who bring every embroidery to life by hand. The handloom weavers whose techniques use 90% less water than mill production. The women whose skill is in every piece we make.

Collection 23 celebrates ten years of that relationship. Marigold, lotus and bougainvillea. The spirit of Rajasthan seen through the lens of a 70s editorial dream.

Marina Cortbawi, Founder

On designing for freedom

"I design for freedom. Which I also happened to be craving in my own life."

Marina spent years in structured fashion. International sales at Oscar de la Renta. Director at Carolina Herrera. She knew how clothes were made and who they were made for. She also knew something was missing: clothes that made women feel at ease, not just dressed.

Merlette began in 2016 with that idea. A New York atelier making garments that feel elegant without effort. That you keep coming back to not because they are trendy but because they make you feel like yourself.

Copywriter's Notes
What changed and why

The Hero

The live Merlette homepage hero is a full-bleed video with "Spring 2026" as the only text and a Shop Now button beneath it. There is no brand voice, no story, nothing that explains why a first-time visitor should care. Marina's own words — "I design for freedom. Which I also happened to be craving in my own life" — are far more powerful than anything currently on the site. This rewrite puts the founding belief on the page where it can do the work it deserves, without disrupting the visual-first layout.

The India Section

The Spring 2026 collection description sits in a video caption on the live site. "A love letter to India, the birthplace of our fabrics, the home of the artisans who bring every embroidery to life" is an extraordinary piece of brand storytelling that most visitors will never read. Merlette's 10th anniversary and the India relationship are the most compelling story the brand has right now. This rewrite gives it a full editorial section — matching the dark, immersive tone of the live site's video treatment — and earns its weight as conversion copy.

The Founder Section

The live About page describes Marina entirely in the third person in press-release language. Marina's actual voice, from interviews, is warm, direct, and specific. This rewrite brings her forward in the first person and tells the story that the About page buries: she came from the most structured houses in fashion — Oscar de la Renta, Carolina Herrera — and left to make something freer. That contrast is what Merlette is. A visitor who understands that story buys differently.

The Newsletter Capture

"Sign up to receive news and special offers" is the current newsletter copy on the live site footer. For a brand whose 23rd collection is described as a love letter to India, whose founder writes a travel guide in the Journal, and whose artisan relationships span three continents, "news and special offers" is a profound mismatch. "Written for the women who want to know more than just what to wear" is the promise the brand already keeps in its Journal. The newsletter copy should say so.

Live copy

A season label and a CTA. No brand voice, no story, no reason for a first-time visitor to feel anything before being asked to shop.
Rewrite

Leads with Marina's founding belief, told through her own story. The visitor understands the brand before they are asked to buy anything.
